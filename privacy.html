Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

At ResumeForge, accessible from resumeforge.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by ResumeForge and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

ResumeForge operates primarily as a client-side application. The data you enter to build your resume (including your name, email, phone number, work experience, education history, and profile photos) is stored locally on your device via browser LocalStorage.

We do not transmit, save, or sync your personal resume contents to any external web servers, unless you explicitly trigger an export to your own computer. Your data remains strictly under your control.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, ResumeForge uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

4. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@resumeforge.com.
